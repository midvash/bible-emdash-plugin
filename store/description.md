**Bible by Midvash** lets you quote the Bible in your EmDash posts without copy-pasting. Open a post, type a reference like `John 3:16`, `Psalm 23` or `Romans 8:28-39`, pick a translation, and the passage is added to your text as a quote, with the reference and the translation's credit.

### What you get in this Marketplace edition

- **Insert verse panel** beside the editor, for posts, pages and projects.
- **23 free Bible translations** in English, Portuguese and Spanish: KJV, Berean Standard Bible (BSB), World English Bible (WEB), ASV, Geneva 1599, Bíblia Livre (Almeida), Reina-Valera 1909 and more.
- **Book names in three languages**: `John 3:16`, `João 3:16` and `Juan 3:16` all work, with common abbreviations (`Jn 3:16`, `1 Cor 13`).
- **Whole chapters or verse ranges**: `Psalm 23` brings the full psalm, `Matthew 5:3-12` brings the Beatitudes.
- **Proper attribution**: each quote ends with the reference and the translation's title and license, which openly licensed (CC BY) translations require. Optionally the reference links to the passage on [midvash.com](https://midvash.com).
- **JSON routes** (`lookup`, `passages`, `versions`) for your theme, and a `scan` MCP tool that finds Bible references in any text.

### What this edition does not do

The hover tooltip (verse preview when a reader points at a reference on your public pages) needs a script on every page. EmDash only lets **trusted** plugins add scripts, so the tooltip is available only in the npm edition. See *Installation* for both editions.
